Who needs a paid account?

Paid accounts
  • GuardianYou manage diabetes for someone you love.
  • Co‑guardianA second guardian for the same person. Each guardian has their own account.
  • IndividualYou manage your own diabetes.
Always free

Everyone who helps.

Grandparents, babysitters, school nurses, coaches, friends, coworkers. They sign in with a code from a Guardian or Individual account. No account to create, nothing to pay.

Who’s behind it

Two founders. One reason.

Managing diabetes shouldn’t mean doing it alone. What began as a way to protect one little girl is now built for everyone living with type 1 or type 2, and the people who help them.

Right now, Glucose Guardian works with Dexcom CGMs only. To connect, turn on Sharing in the Dexcom app and sign in with your Dexcom username.
