Built‑in safeguards

Careful by design.

The calculator follows the same principles as commercial bolus calculators — and adds guardrails for the moments when families are most likely to make a mistake.

No dose stacking.

For a while after a dose, any extra correction is held — so reopening the calculator ten minutes later won’t suggest more. Carbs are always covered.

A ceiling on every suggestion.

Each suggestion is capped based on body weight. If the math goes past it, the app says so and asks you to check with your care team.

Lows come first.

When glucose is low, the app points you to fast‑acting carbs. Below target, the correction is left out.

Plain‑language warnings.

Very high? Verify with a finger stick and check ketones. Earlier insulin not working? Check the site or pen.

Learns, never overrides.

If your logged doses consistently differ from suggestions, a small pattern adjustment appears as its own line. Your settings never change on their own.

Basal is its own thing.

Long‑acting insulin isn’t dosed from carbs. Glucose Guardian keeps meal math and basal doses separate.

Questions? Answers.

What is Glucose Guardian?+

An app for families and people managing diabetes. It shows live Dexcom readings, estimates carbs from photos, barcodes, or search, suggests insulin doses with the math laid out, sends alerts, and keeps co‑guardians, caregivers, the school nurse, and the doctor on the same page.

Which glucose monitors does it work with?+

Dexcom, through Dexcom Share. Sign in once and readings sync automatically. [OTHER CGMS — COMING LATER?]

What’s the difference between a co‑guardian and a caregiver?+

Co‑guardians have their own account and full access — they can log, fix, and dose from anywhere. Caregivers use a code with no account, and only get the permissions and hours you choose.

Does a babysitter or teacher need an account?+

No. Create a caregiver code, choose what they can see and do and when, and share it by text or QR. They enter it on the sign‑in screen. Retire it any time.

Who can change insulin settings?+

Only the primary guardian. Doctors can propose changes from the portal, but nothing changes until a guardian approves it.

Does it replace my doctor or care team?+

No. Glucose Guardian provides estimates only and does not replace medical advice. Always follow your doctor’s instructions.

Questions from schools.

Which CGMs does it work with?+

Right now, Glucose Guardian works with Dexcom CGMs only. The family turns on Sharing in the Dexcom app and connects their Dexcom account, and the readings show up on your menu.

Does each nurse need their own account?+

Yes. Each nurse, teacher, or aide signs up as a Caregiver and adds the students they look after.

Where do the access codes come from?+

From each student’s guardian, in the Care Circle section of their Dashboard. Codes are 8 characters and can be shared by text or QR.

Can a nurse change a student’s insulin settings?+

No. Insulin settings belong to the primary guardian. A nurse can use the calculator only if the family allows it.

What happens after school hours?+

If the family set a schedule, the student shows as Locked on your menu until the next window opens.

Does it replace our school’s diabetes care plan?+

Questions from clinicians.

Where does the glucose data come from?+

From the family’s Dexcom CGM. Right now, Glucose Guardian works with Dexcom CGMs only.

Who can sign in to the portal?+

Clinicians with a license key or invite code from their organization. The demo is open to anyone, but it only contains made‑up sample patients.

How do I add a patient?+

The family shares a Doctor Code from their Glucose Guardian app. You enter it in the portal and the patient appears on your list.

Can I change a patient’s settings?+

You can propose carb‑ratio, correction, and target changes. They take effect only after the caregiver confirms in the app.

Can I message the school nurse?+

Yes. Parents and caregivers tagged as School Nurse in the care circle can be messaged from the patient’s chart.

Does it replace clinical judgment?+

No. Glucose Guardian provides estimates and summaries only and does not replace medical advice.
